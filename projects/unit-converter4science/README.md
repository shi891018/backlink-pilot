## Working rules

1. Use the homepage for general product and startup directories.
2. Use a relevant `/en/{category}` page only when the directory or article is specifically about that unit category.
3. Use `/en/about` for methodology, accuracy, and educational-resource contexts; do not make unsupported certification or medical claims.
4. Mention the controlled AI explanations only on directories that genuinely accept AI-assisted tools, and keep the homepage as the landing URL unless the AI feature is the subject.
5. Record every attempt in [submissions.md](submissions.md) before submitting again.
6. Skip paid placements and reciprocal footer-link requirements unless explicitly approved.
7. For blog comments, contribute a genuinely useful answer first and submit only to closely relevant articles; never bulk-post URLs.
