# Article drafts

This directory holds original, publication-ready outreach articles and pitch
emails. Drafts should be adapted to each publication rather than sent as a
bulk duplicate.

## Suggested first topics

1. **Why Showing the Conversion Formula Matters in Science and Engineering**
   Explain how readable factors and intermediate steps help students and
   practitioners check a result.
2. **Metric to Imperial Conversions: A Practical Reference for Everyday Work**
   Cover length, mass, area, and volume with careful notes about US and UK
   units.
3. **How to Check a Unit Conversion Before It Enters a Lab Notebook**
   Give a short, repeatable verification workflow for research and education.
4. **Temperature, Pressure, and Concentration: Where Context Changes the Result**
   Show why definitions and assumptions should be stated without making medical
   or safety-critical claims.
5. **Teaching Unit Conversion With Formulas Instead of Black-Box Answers**
   Position the tool as a supplementary classroom reference, not a replacement
   for instruction.

## Editorial constraints

- Prefer one natural homepage or relevant category link per article.
- Do not claim official certification, laboratory validation, or guaranteed
  scientific accuracy unless separately documented.
- State assumptions for approximate units such as average month/year, Mach, or
  speed of sound when they are relevant.
- Avoid medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical advice.
- Disclose the relationship to the project when a publication requests it.
