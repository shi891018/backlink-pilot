# Unit Converter for Science backlink outreach plan

## Project summary

- Website: https://www.unitconv.top
- Product: free online unit converter for science, engineering, education, and everyday use
- Core differentiators: formulas, conversion factors, readable derivation steps, multilingual support, and a privacy-first workflow
- Supported languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, and Italian
- Supported categories: length, mass, time, velocity, area, volume, temperature, energy, force, pressure, and concentration
- Source repository: https://github.com/shi891018/unit-converter4science-nextjs

## Recommended outreach sequence

### 1. Product and resource directories

Start with free, relevant directories that accept utilities, education, science,
engineering, calculators, or productivity tools. Use the homepage as the
landing URL. Skip dead sites, paid-only placements, and listings that require a
permanent reciprocal footer link.

### 2. Science and education resources

Prioritize physics, chemistry, engineering, mathematics, teaching, lab-skills,
and STEM resource sites. Offer a concise, useful explanation or reference
article first. Use `/en/about` for methodology-focused contexts and a category
page only when it directly matches the article.

### 3. Original guest articles

Prepare two versions of each strong idea: a 700–1,000-word practical article
and a 1,000–1,500-word educational article. Each publication should receive a
different introduction and examples. Include at most one natural project link
unless the publication explicitly allows more.

### 4. Community participation

Answer questions on programming, science, engineering, education, and maker
communities where unit conversion is already relevant. Do not create new
accounts for immediate link drops, and do not post identical comments across
sites.

## Suggested anchor language

Use branded or descriptive anchors such as `Unit Converter for Science`, `free
scientific unit converter`, `unit conversion reference`, or `conversion formula
reference`. Avoid exact-match repetition such as `best unit converter` on every
submission.

## Verification checklist

For every target, record the official submission URL, verification date, cost,
landing URL, link location, content requirements, CAPTCHA/login status, and
final result in [submissions.md](submissions.md). Never count a submission as
live until the public page or an official confirmation supports that status.
