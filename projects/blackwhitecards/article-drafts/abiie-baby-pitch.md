Hello Abiie team,

I’m writing on behalf of the Black & White Baby Cards Editorial Team to propose an original guest post for the Abiie infant-care community:

## Proposed title

**A Calm 5-Minute Visual-Play Routine for Newborns**

## Proposed article

The article would give parents and caregivers a simple, low-preparation routine for supervised visual play during the first six months. It would cover how to choose an alert, comfortable moment; introduce one clear high-contrast pattern; keep the viewing distance and movement gentle; notice when a baby has had enough; and adapt the activity as the baby grows. The tone would be practical, reassuring, and focused on connection rather than performance.

The article would also make clear that high-contrast cards are play materials, not medical devices or developmental tests. It would encourage families to follow their baby’s cues and speak with a qualified clinician about individual vision or development concerns. The piece would be approximately 1,000–1,200 words, use clear subheadings, and include citations to reputable parent-health resources where appropriate.

## Why it fits Abiie readers

Abiie’s audience includes parents and caregivers looking for safe, useful ways to make everyday infant care more comfortable and engaging. This topic complements that mission with a calm activity that needs very little equipment and can fit into an ordinary home routine. It is educational rather than sales-focused.

We can provide three original supporting images for editorial review if the pitch is accepted. The images show simple black-and-white card patterns, a supervised caregiver-and-newborn scene, and a tidy card setup.
